Literature Review

Derivation of the action

  • N. Ishibashi, H. Kawai, Y. Kitazawa, A. Tsuchiya, "Large-N reduced models as superstring", Nucl.Phys.B 498 (1997) 467-491

    • The original paper that proposed the IKKT matrix model as a non-perturbative definition of the type IIB superstring theory. They showed that the action of the IKKT matrix model can be derived formally from (1) 10D N=1 super Yang-Mills theory via large-N reduction and (2) type IIB Nambu-Goto action in the Schild gauge via matrix regularization.
    They showed a complete set of symmetries, including 0D version of N=2 SUSY and SU(N) gauge symmetry, SO(9,1) symmetry, and translational symmetry (although didn't specified explicitly, this symmetry showed itself when the discussion of the trace part was carried out by mentioning that the trace part (a shift by an identity matrix) does not appear in the action.).
    They also discussed the various interactions between D-objects, which are represented by block-diagonal matrices. Among such a discussion, they showed that graviton exchange between D-strings can be obtained from matrix framework.
    They discussed the first version of the argument of how the spacetime is an emergent entity in IKKT matrix model. First, they computed the effective action between two diagonal blocks, and found that they are weakly interaction ~r^(-8). With the picture of block-diagonals as D-objects in mind, and that this means that, if D-objects are far apart, they are independent of each other (cluster property). This cluster property is what gives the trace parts the interpretation of the center of mass of D-objects. This means that there is something in this model that can represent spacetime coordinates, and therefore the interpretation of what we called earlier as 0D version of N=2 SUSY is truly allowed.

  • E. Witten, "Bound States Of Strings and p-Branes", Nucl.Phys.B 460 (1996) 335-350

    • First, the (p+1)D worldvolume theory of N Dp-branes is equivalent to the 10D SU(N) super Yang-Mills theory reduced to p+1 dimensions, which gives Dp-branes picture to the reduced model. Then, he considered p=-1 and p=0 case, where he derived the same action as those of the IKKT matrix model and the BFSS matrix model. ***It is important not to mistaken this paper as the first proposal of the IKKT matrix model because this paper only wrote down the action and some connection to type IIB superstring via D0-branes picture, while the original IKKT matrix model paper was the first to provide in-depth investigation into various aspects of this model in terms of what physical properties can be extracted as mentioned above.
