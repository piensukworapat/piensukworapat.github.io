Welcome!

The IKKT matrix model has been around since 1996, when it proposed as a nonperturbative construction of superstring theories. Since then, there have been many developments in many aspects accross various fields such as emergent spacetime, particle physics, noncommutative field theory, and noncommutative geometry. There is without a doubt that the IKKT matrix model is indeed a serious candidate for the complete quantum theory of gravity, or even the theory of everything. However, similar to other revolutionary ideas, it is very important to provide as much evidence as we can to establish this fact on a firm ground.

We believe that, behind any developments, it is important to have a good background knowledge of their respective fields. This website is created exactly for that reason. We want it to serve not only as a ground of established knowledge for experienced researchers to build their ideas on but also as a learning space for students and new researchers to get to know the IKKT matrix model. We hope that ultimately this website will help push the boundary of what we know about our universe and spacetime.
